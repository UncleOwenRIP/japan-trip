# Kyoto & Tokyo Trip Planner

A one-page, phone-friendly trip planner for Nov 10–20, 2026: day-by-day plans you can swap, breakfast/lunch/dinner picks, a street map per day, and photos.

- Street map: [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com) with © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO tiles
- Photos: lead images from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons
- Your edits are saved in your browser (localStorage) on each device

Open it at the GitHub Pages link for this repo, then use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** to open it like an app.
